\section{Primary --- Indian official statistics}

\begin{enumerate}[label={[\arabic*]}]

press release of 12 February 2026). \textbf{The single most important source for this
project.} Establishes: base 2024$=$100; HCES 2023--24 weights; COICOP 2018 with 12
divisions / 43 groups / 92 classes / 162 sub-classes; 358 weighted items; 1{,}465 rural and
1{,}395 urban markets across 434 towns plus 12 online markets; \textbf{Jevons} for
elementary and \textbf{Young/Modified Laspeyres} for higher-level indices; weekly online
price collection; \textbf{``Airfares are collected through well-known online platforms''};
division-wise weights including Transport at 8.796\% combined; the linking-factor formula and

4.0\% of rural and 10.5\% of urban household expenditure --- the justification for the

\section{Primary --- Indian civil aviation}

\begin{enumerate}[label={[\arabic*]},start=10]
\item DGCA, monthly \emph{Traffic Report} (domestic). Passengers carried, market share,
passenger load factor, cancellations and causes, complaints, on-time performance.
Establishes: 864.04 lakh passengers Jan--Jun 2026 ($+$1.44\% y-o-y); 984.03 lakh Jan--Jul
2026; IndiGo 66.3\% (June) and 67.4\% (July) domestic share; Air India Group $\approx$24\%;
Akasa 5.5\%; SpiceJet 1.6\%; PLFs Akasa 92.2\% / SpiceJet 87.8\% / AI Group 85.5\% / IndiGo
85.1\%; cancellation rate 0.63\%.\\
\refurl{https://www.dgca.gov.in}

\item Rajya Sabha, reply of the Minister of State for Civil Aviation, 8 December 2025.
Establishes the \textbf{Tariff Monitoring Unit}: set up 2010, monitors \textbf{78 selected
routes}, \emph{monthly}, \emph{manually, using airlines' websites}, covering
\textbf{$\approx$27\% of domestic traffic}; airfares remain deregulated under \textbf{Rule
135 of the Aircraft Rules, 1937}.

\item Ministry of Civil Aviation, reply to Parliament, July 2026. Establishes a
\textbf{20.5\% increase in average airfare across 72 domestic sectors} comparing June 2026
with March 2025, and that \textbf{ATF is 35--40\% of airline operating cost} with prices up
over 100\% on the West Asia conflict.

\item Parliamentary Standing Committee on Transport, Tourism and Culture, \emph{375th
Report: Demands for Grants (2025--26) of the Ministry of Civil Aviation}. Finds the DGCA
lacks capacity to proactively monitor airfare trends; recommends an AI-driven predictive
monitoring system (``AirPrice Guardian''), a public \textbf{``Pricing Transparency Index''},
and an \textbf{``Airfare Vigil''} consumer app. \textbf{The basis of USP 10.}

\item Reporting on the DGCA's December 2024 request to airlines for two years of
ticket-level data (booking date, fare charged, base fare) and the Federation of Indian
Airlines' refusal on commercial-confidentiality grounds (letter of 11 March 2025).
\textbf{The evidence that the booking lead-time distribution is genuinely unobtainable.}

\item AERA tariff orders (User Development Fee). \url{https://aera.gov.in}
\item Airports Authority of India traffic statistics. \url{https://www.aai.aero}
\item PPAC, Ministry of Petroleum \& Natural Gas --- ATF and fuel prices.
\refurl{https://ppac.gov.in}
\end{enumerate}

\section{International methodology --- index numbers and alternative data}

\begin{enumerate}[label={[\arabic*]},start=18]
\item IMF/ILO/OECD/UNECE/Eurostat/World Bank, \emph{Consumer Price Index Manual: Concepts
and Methods} (2020). The canonical reference. Elementary formulae, axiomatic tests,
Laspeyres/Paasche/Fisher/T\"ornqvist, Young and Lowe. \url{https://www.imf.org}

\item W.\ E.\ Diewert, \emph{Scanner Data, Elementary Price Indexes and the Chain Drift
Problem} --- companion chapter to the CPI Manual. Multilateral indices (GEKS, GEKS-T\"ornqvist
/ CCDI), Time Product Dummy and Weighted TPD, missing-product handling. Explicitly cites
\textbf{airlines and hotels as the dynamic-pricing case} motivating multilateral methods.\\
\refurl{https://www.imf.org/~/media/Files/Data/CPI/companion-publication/chapter-6-chain-drift-problem-and-multilateral-indices.ashx}

\item Eurostat, \emph{Guidelines on the use of web scraped data in the HICP} (November
2020). Establishes that weights are typically unavailable for web-scraped data, that fixed-base
Jevons is the standard approach, that \textbf{GEKS-Jevons and Time Product Dummy} are the
multilateral options, and that Dutot implicitly weights by base-period price level.\\
\refurl{https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/272892/12032198/Guidelines-web-scraping-HICP-11-2020.pdf}

\item ONS, \emph{Aggregate index of air fares methodology}. \textbf{The single best
international precedent for this project.} Establishes ONS's advance-collection windows
(long-haul 6/3/1 months; short-haul 3/1; domestic 1 month), collection on a commercially
confidential ``index day'' (usually the 2nd or 3rd Tuesday), inclusion of return legs, and
--- critically --- that \textbf{air fare changes are recorded in the CPI in the month the
flight departs, not when the ticket is bought}. \textbf{The basis of USP 3.}\\
\refurl{https://www.ons.gov.uk/aboutus/transparencyandgovernance/freedomofinformationfoi/aggregateindexofairfaresmethodology}

\item ONS, \emph{Domestic, European and long-haul airfares consumer prices sub-indices}
(ad hoc release, Jan 2017 -- Feb 2025). Shows a national statistical institute publishing
airfare sub-indices \emph{by advance-purchase window} --- direct precedent for the APIx
$\tau$ dimension.\\
\refurl{https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/adhocs/2716domesticeuropeanandlonghaulairfaresconsumerpricessubindicesjanuary2017tofebruary2025}

\item ONS, \emph{New index number methods in consumer price statistics} (2020). Scores
methods against a framework; finds Quality-adjusted Geary--Khamis best where expenditure
information exists and recommends \textbf{GEKS-Jevons in its absence} --- i.e.\ the APIx case.\\
\refurl{https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/articles/newindexnumbermethodsinconsumerpricestatistics/2020-09-01}

\item ONS, \emph{Introducing multilateral index methods into consumer price statistics}.
GEKS-T\"ornqvist with a 25-month window and a \textbf{mean splice}; the splicing taxonomy.\\
\refurl{https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/methodologies/introducingmultilateralindexmethodsintoconsumerpricestatistics}

\item ONS, \emph{How multilateral index methods help us understand grocery scanner data}
(2026). Confirms GEKS-T\"ornqvist already in use for rail fares and second-hand cars, and
adopted for grocery scanner data from March 2026.\\
\refurl{https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/methodologies/howmultilateralindexmethodshelpusunderstandgroceryscannerdata}

\item ONS, \emph{Research indices using web scraped price data} (2017 update). Bilateral vs.\
multilateral taxonomy; the CLIP index; GEKS sensitivity to atypical prices.

\item K.\ Van Loon (Statbel), \emph{Scanner data and web scraping in the Belgian CPI} (2020).
Practical implementation notes; GEKS-T\"ornqvist, 25-month rolling window, half splice.

\item National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, \emph{Modernizing the
Consumer Price Index for the 21st Century}. Chain drift, multilateral methods, and the
concordance problem between non-survey data hierarchies and CPI nomenclature.\\
\refurl{https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/26485/chapter/4}

\item J.\ Lent and A.\ H.\ Dorfman, \emph{A transaction price index for air travel},
\emph{Monthly Labor Review}, BLS, June 2005. Why listed fares differ from transaction prices,
and the imputation and matching problems specific to air travel.\\
\refurl{https://www.bls.gov/opub/mlr/2005/06/art2full.pdf}

\item BLS, \emph{Import and Export Air Passenger Fares Price Indexes} fact sheet.\\
\refurl{https://www.bls.gov/mxp/publications/factsheets/air-passenger-facts.pdf}

\item BLS, CPI for All Urban Consumers: Airline Fares (series \code{CUSR0000SETG01}), via
FRED --- a 1989--2026 comparator series.\\
\refurl{https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CUSR0000SETG01}

\item UN Statistics Division, \emph{Classification of Individual Consumption According to
Purpose (COICOP) 2018}. The classification MoSPI adopted for CPI 2024; source of the 07.3.3
code.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Legal --- data collection in India}

\begin{enumerate}[label={[\arabic*]},start=33]
\item \emph{Information Technology Act, 2000}, ss.\ 43 and 66 --- compensation and criminal
liability for accessing, downloading, copying or extracting data from a computer resource
without the owner's permission; s.\ 43 also covers disruption.

\item \emph{Copyright Act, 1957}, s.\ 13(1)(a); \emph{Eastern Book Company v.\ D.B.\ Modak}
(Supreme Court of India) --- the ``skill and judgment'' standard under which a compilation
attracts copyright.

\item \emph{Indian Contract Act, 1872} --- terms of use as an enforceable contract.

\item \emph{Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023} --- engages only where personal data
is processed. \textbf{Not applicable to APIx}, which collects no personal data.

\item Reply of the Minister of State for Electronics and IT, Rajya Sabha (August 2024) ---
the Government's position that scraping publicly available data may breach s.\ 43 of the IT
Act.

\item \emph{hiQ Labs v.\ LinkedIn} (US 9th Cir., 2022) --- scraping public pages is not
``unauthorised access'' under the CFAA.

\item \emph{Meta v.\ Bright Data} (2024) --- terms bind account holders; \textbf{logged-out}
public scraping is materially more defensible. Directly motivates the unauthenticated-session
requirement in the APIx product key.

\item \emph{eBay v.\ Bidder's Edge} (N.D.\ Cal., 2000) --- trespass to chattels at extreme
request volumes; the origin of the rate-limiting-as-legal-defence argument.
\end{enumerate}
