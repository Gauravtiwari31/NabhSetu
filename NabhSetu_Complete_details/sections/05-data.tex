\section{Designing the route basket}

\subsection{Selection rule}

The PS names an illustrative basket (\rt{del}--\rt{bom}, \rt{del}--\rt{blr},
\rt{bom}--\rt{blr}, \rt{del}--\rt{ccu}, \rt{blr}--\rt{hyd}, \rt{maa}--\rt{del}). Do not
just hard-code it. Derive it, then show that the derivation reproduces the PS list --- which
simultaneously satisfies the PS and demonstrates method.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Rank all directional city-pairs by annual passengers $Q_r$ from AAI/DGCA sector data.
\item Select the top $M$ routes such that cumulative coverage exceeds a target:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{B} \;=\; \arg\min_{M}\;\left\{ M \;:\; \frac{\sum_{r=1}^{M} Q_{(r)}}{\sum_{\text{all}} Q_r} \;\geq\; \theta \right\}
\end{equation}
with $\theta = 0.60$ for the hackathon build and $\theta = 0.80$ documented as the
production target. Report the achieved coverage as a headline metric --- and note that the
DGCA TMU's 78 routes cover about 27\%, so beating that number is a concrete, quotable win.
\item \textbf{Stratify, do not just take the head.} Force representation across:
metro--metro, metro--tier-2, tier-2--tier-2, and \textbf{UDAN/RCS routes} (which are
subsidised and price-capped, and therefore behave completely differently --- including them
is a genuine methodological point about heterogeneity).
\item Force geographic spread: North, West, South, East, North-East. A basket that is all
golden-quadrilateral routes is not a national index.
\item Re-select annually; publish the basket and the selection date. Mid-series basket
changes must be linked, not spliced silently.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Design decisions worth defending}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Quotes are immutable.} No \code{UPDATE} on \code{fact\_fare\_quote}, ever.
Corrections are new rows with a supersession pointer. Official statistics is an
append-only discipline; a mutable price table is disqualifying.
\item \textbf{\code{quality\_flag} and \code{disposition} are separate.} The flag says what
the detector found; the disposition says what the pipeline did about it. Conflating them
loses the ability to re-run cleaning with different thresholds.
\item \textbf{\code{raw\_hash}} is the SHA-256 of the canonicalised raw payload, stored so a
number can be traced to the exact bytes it came from without retaining the full payload
indefinitely.
\item \textbf{\code{method\_version} and \code{weights\_version}} are stamped on every index
value. When the methodology changes, old numbers remain reproducible under the old version.
\item \textbf{TimescaleDB hypertable} on \code{collected\_at\_utc} with 1-day chunks,
compression after 7 days, continuous aggregates for the daily and weekly rollups.
\item \textbf{Both UTC and IST} are stored. Fares are quoted against local departure times;
collection scheduling is in IST; analysis needs monotone UTC. Storing one and deriving the
other has caused more price-statistics bugs than any other single decision.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data quality contract}

Implement as executable assertions (Great Expectations, or plain \code{pytest} against a
\code{pandera} schema). The suite runs on every ingestion batch and blocks publication on
failure.

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{3.4cm} X L{2.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\bhead{Check class} & \bhead{Assertion} & \bhead{On failure} \\
\midrule
Schema & Types, nullability, enum domains for cabin/disposition & Block \\
Range & $0 <$ base fare $<$ total fare; \code{apw\_days} $\in\mathcal{T}$; GST $\approx$ rate $\times$ base & Block \\
Arithmetic & $|P^{\text{total}} - \sum \text{components}| \le$ INR 1 & Block \\
Freshness & Every basket route has $\ge 1$ quote in the last 26 h & Alert \\
Completeness & Cell fill rate $\ge 80\%$ of expected & Alert; suppress cell \\
Uniqueness & No duplicate $(\kappa, t, \text{source})$ & Block \\
Distributional & Cell median log-fare within $4\sigma$ of trailing 28-day median & Quarantine \\
Referential & Every \code{provenance\_id} resolves; hash chain intact & Block \\
Cross-source & Same flight from two sources differs by $\le 5\%$ ex-convenience-fee & Alert \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\begin{keybox}
\bhead{The cross-source check is a hidden USP.} When two independent sources quote the same
flight, the difference is an estimate of \emph{measurement error} in your own instrument.
Publishing a measurement-error series --- ``our quotes agree to within $x\%$'' --- is
something no competing team will think of, and it is precisely the sort of thing a
statistical office cares about.
\end{keybox}
