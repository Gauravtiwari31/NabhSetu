Availability adjustment

The single most defensible criticism of a naive fare index: when cheap buckets sell out during a surge, the only quote still observable never changed price, so a strictly matched index reports zero inflation through exactly the episode consumers feel as a fare explosion. Iadj = ImatchedA · ILAF1−A — at A=1 it reduces exactly to the matched index.

Lead-time elasticity η(τ)

η(τ) = ∂ln p/∂ln τ = β₁ + 2β₂·ln τ, from a pooled log-linear fit with route, carrier, day-of-week and month fixed effects and standard errors clustered by flight. Because β₂ enters, the elasticity varies with lead time — and the trough τ* = exp(−β₁/2β₂) is the most press-friendly number the system produces.

Fare component decomposition

UDF, ASF, RCS levy and GST are administered prices loaded from AERA / BCAS / GST Council notifications into effective-dated SCD tables — never scraped. The base fare is the residual, reconciled to within ₹1. The OTA convenience fee is not a fare: it differs across OTAs for an identical seat, so it is published as a separate distribution cost.

Back-test against official CPI

Formal agreement statistics, not an eyeballed overlay — Pearson and Spearman on levels and on month-on-month changes, Bland–Altman bias and limits of agreement, directional agreement, and RMSE against a random-walk benchmark. Reported whether or not they flatter the index, and refused outright when the overlap is too short to support them.
